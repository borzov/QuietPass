Support

Using QuietPass

  1. Requires macOS 15 or later and Safari 18 or later. Open QuietPass, choose Open Safari Settings and enable QuietPass under Extensions.
  2. Click the key icon in Safari. Each opening creates one main password and five independent alternatives. Length, complexity, special characters and hyphens are saved automatically.
  3. Copy a password, or select an editable field on a page, reopen QuietPass and choose Insert. Grant access to that page if Safari requests it. The extension does not submit forms.

Hyphens and length

With hyphens enabled, two hyphens divide the password into three balanced groups. The selected length of 8–64 characters includes both separators.

History and retention

History contains only successfully copied or inserted passwords. Open History or Settings to load it. Choose a retention period or clear history with confirmation. Records stay separate for each Safari profile; expired records are removed when history is accessed.

If insertion is unavailable

Select a normal editable text or password field and try again. Safari restricts access to some pages and frames. Copy remains available when insertion cannot be performed.

Private windows

In private windows, password generation and copying are available. History and insertion are disabled.

The containing app

After opening Safari settings or closing its window, the containing app exits. The extension works without it running. Keep QuietPass.app installed.
