Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

QuietPass is a Safari password generator maintained by Maksim Borzov. It generates new passwords on your Mac. The app does not transmit passwords, browsing history, website addresses or account names to the developer, and has no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

Local storage and deletion

Generation preferences are stored locally. Only passwords you successfully copy or insert are added to history, which is stored locally in the macOS Keychain and separated by Safari profile. QuietPass does not sync history through iCloud or send it to a server. Unused generated passwords are not recorded.

In Settings, choose not to save history or keep records for 1, 7, 30 or 90 days; the default is 7 days. The period runs from the time a password is used. Expired records are removed on the next history access. History is not accessed just to open the generator. Extending the period does not restore expired records. You can clear history, or turn it off and delete existing records, after confirmation in the extension.

Copying and insertion

Copy places the selected password on the system clipboard. Clipboard access and any system clipboard synchronization are controlled by macOS and your settings. Insert passes only the selected password to the editable field you chose on the current page. QuietPass does not submit the form or collect the page contents.

Contacting support

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to include. This is used to reply and resolve your request, and retained only as needed for that purpose and any applicable obligations. Contact max.borzov@me.com about a support message or its deletion. Passwords are not needed to provide support.

This website

These pages contain no analytics scripts or submission forms. They are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes; its handling of website data is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Opening the extension does not visit this website automatically.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if QuietPass’s data practices change. The date above identifies this version. For questions about privacy, contact max.borzov@me.com.
